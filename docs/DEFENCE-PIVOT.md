# Collective defence pivot — 2026-11-09

Norevia is a Cold Code Labs research project on **Social Immunity for Agentic Systems**. The scope is information integrity in LLM-based agents: poisoned evidence, prompt injection, compromised shared state, selective containment and verified recovery. This is a research programme, not demonstrated security efficacy.

## Public content

- `/`: interactive mechanism sketch and four proposed defence stages.
- `/defence/`: threat assumptions, conventional baselines, metrics, evidence status and source-review limits.
- `/explore/`: relevance filters independent of evidence level and hypothesis status; classifications are also in `/archive.json`.
- `hypotheses/H-D01.md` through `H-D14.md`: new, open engineering hypotheses with predictions, refutation criteria and conventional baselines. No experiment or spend is triggered by adding a card.
- Historical experiments, simulations, flags and theory retain their original scope. Coding/synthetic findings must not be presented as cyber-defence results.

## Illustration rules

The landing-page sketch has 16 ring nodes and four cross-links. A step copies the exposed claim to immediate neighbours. Quarantine traces and blocks all exposed nodes. Recovery assumes a trusted external check rejects the claim and clears every copy. This deliberately perfect tracing/checking illustrates a mechanism; it does not predict real detector performance, costs or protection. Counts come from the illustrated state, never from an experiment.

## Nature-first presentation

The landing page begins with a schematic ant colony and three source-linked biological mechanisms: contact-network plasticity (Stroeymeyt 2018), honeybee stop signals (Tan 2016, with its figure-quality correction), and destructive brood disinfection (Pull 2018). Each pairs the named-species observation with an expandable engineering hypothesis and refutation test. The agent-network sketch follows these observations. Drawings are illustrations, not biological simulations, and recovery remains an engineering question. Blockchain is not introduced as a core mechanism.

## Three lenses

The LP presents Unseen (microscopic biological recognition), Seen (social-insect immunity, the central thesis), and Beyond (physical shielding). Magnetosphere, atmosphere and heliosphere are protective physical phenomena, not adaptive immune agents. Constellations are sky regions, not defensive collectives. Each lens separates observation, engineering hypothesis and rejection criterion; none establishes agent-system efficacy. Overview sources are linked alongside primary biological studies. The drawings are schematic and not to scale.
