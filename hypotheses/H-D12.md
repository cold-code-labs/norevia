# Sentinel division of labour

**Status: open.** Proposed engineering hypothesis. No defensive experiment has run.

## Variable
share of agents carrying costly defences: all / sentinel fraction f / none, with the rest protected by sentinel verdicts

## Outcomes
cost per protected task, injection success rate

## Prediction
a small sentinel fraction protects at lower cost than guarding every agent, until sentinels become the single point of failure

## Refutation criterion
per-agent guardrails on every agent are cheaper at equal protection, or sentinel compromise causes collective failure

## Strong conventional baseline
per-agent guardrails; a shared gateway or proxy

## Evaluation scope and limits
Synthetic or licensed cases in owned or isolated environments. Match cost and legitimate-task utility; report false positives, missed incidents and verifier error. No third-party targets. Detailed cases, stopping rules and an execution budget must be pre-registered before a run. This research proposal grants no operational permissions.
