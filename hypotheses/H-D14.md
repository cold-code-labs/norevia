# Signals under attack

**Status: open.** Proposed engineering hypothesis. No defensive experiment has run.

## Variable
signal handling: open / authenticated and rate-capped; attacker model (in an owned sandbox) forging alarms, flooding, or triggering inhibition

## Outcomes
attacker cost to cause X% downtime or to silence a real alarm

## Prediction
unauthenticated alarm and inhibitory signals are a denial-of-service lever; authentication with rate caps removes it at small cost

## Refutation criterion
the mechanisms are not exploitable at realistic cost, or authentication erases the benefit shown in H-D04

## Strong conventional baseline
authenticated messaging, rate limiting, alert flood protection

## Evaluation scope and limits
Synthetic or licensed cases in owned or isolated environments. Match cost and legitimate-task utility; report false positives, missed incidents and verifier error. No third-party targets. Detailed cases, stopping rules and an execution budget must be pre-registered before a run. This research proposal grants no operational permissions.
