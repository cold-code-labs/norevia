# Risk-adjusted recognition

**Status: open.** Proposed engineering hypothesis. No defensive experiment has run.

## Variable
investigation depth and acceptance threshold fixed vs scaled by context risk (fixed authorisation limits in both)

## Outcomes
expected loss = missed-threat cost + false-alarm cost + investigation cost, at matched spend; plus robustness to an attacker who inflates apparent pressure

## Prediction
adaptive depth beats a fixed threshold when risk context shifts, and loses when risk is constant

## Refutation criterion
a fixed threshold tuned on the same period matches within the CI, or an adversary can push the system into a lax mode

## Strong conventional baseline
risk-based authentication; fixed-threshold triage; tiered SOAR playbooks

## Evaluation scope and limits
Synthetic or licensed cases in owned or isolated environments. Match cost and legitimate-task utility; report false positives, missed incidents and verifier error. No third-party targets. Detailed cases, stopping rules and an execution budget must be pre-registered before a run. This research proposal grants no operational permissions.
