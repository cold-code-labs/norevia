# Defensive plasticity

**Status: open.** Proposed engineering hypothesis. No defensive experiment has run.

## Variable
policy for reorganising read-edges after threat evidence: fixed topology / global isolate / targeted rewiring of the implicated edges

## Outcomes
poisoned-evidence reach (agents whose final decision depends on the poisoned item) and accepted tasks per US$, reported as a Pareto frontier

## Prediction
targeted rewiring reaches lower reach than fixed topology at less throughput loss than global isolation

## Refutation criterion
static zero-trust with per-edge provenance gating gives the same or a better frontier, or global isolation dominates

## Strong conventional baseline
zero trust + least privilege + static segmentation; whole-component quarantine

## Evaluation scope and limits
Synthetic or licensed cases in owned or isolated environments. Match cost and legitimate-task utility; report false positives, missed incidents and verifier error. No third-party targets. Detailed cases, stopping rules and an execution budget must be pre-registered before a run. This research proposal grants no operational permissions.
