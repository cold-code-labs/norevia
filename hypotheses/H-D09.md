# Memory quarantine and verified reuse

**Status: open.** Proposed engineering hypothesis. No defensive experiment has run.

## Variable
shared-memory policy: unverified persistent / TTL / provenance + re-verification before reuse / + quarantine

## Outcomes
poison persistence (rounds until a poisoned entry stops influencing decisions), poisoned-write success rate, legitimate reuse rate (links H-19, H-25)

## Prediction
provenance with re-verification shortens poison persistence at small cost in reuse

## Refutation criterion
TTL alone gives the same persistence reduction, or reuse falls below stateless agents

## Strong conventional baseline
signed memory entries, TTL, write audit logs, human review of persisted entries

## Evaluation scope and limits
Synthetic or licensed cases in owned or isolated environments. Match cost and legitimate-task utility; report false positives, missed incidents and verifier error. No third-party targets. Detailed cases, stopping rules and an execution budget must be pre-registered before a run. This research proposal grants no operational permissions.
