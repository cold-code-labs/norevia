# Bounded recovery and resolution

**Status: open.** Proposed engineering hypothesis. No defensive experiment has run.

## Variable
remediation: unbounded auto-remediation / bounded with snapshot, approval gate for destructive actions / bounded + stop on verified resolution (extends H-14, H-24, wound_resolution)

## Outcomes
reversible-action ratio, recurrence, remediation loops, false all-clear rate, cost

## Prediction
stop-on-verified-resolution cuts cost (LAB-004 saw x1.43 at N = 1,000) without raising recurrence; destructive steps only with authorisation and backup

## Refutation criterion
false all-clear rate rises, or a SOAR playbook with approval and rollback matches at lower cost

## Strong conventional baseline
SOAR playbook with approval gates and rollback

## Evaluation scope and limits
Synthetic or licensed cases in owned or isolated environments. Match cost and legitimate-task utility; report false positives, missed incidents and verifier error. No third-party targets. Detailed cases, stopping rules and an execution budget must be pre-registered before a run. This research proposal grants no operational permissions.
