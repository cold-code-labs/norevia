# Prompt-injection propagation

**Status: open.** Proposed engineering hypothesis. No defensive experiment has run.

## Variable
read-neighbourhood size k, trust gating, hop limit

## Outcomes
effective reproduction number R_eff of an injected instruction and final infected fraction (extends H-22)

## Prediction
reducing k and gating by provenance pushes R_eff below 1; ungated broadcast pushes it above

## Refutation criterion
R_eff unaffected by topology (the injection spreads or dies by model compliance alone), or gating a guardrail classifier does as well

## Strong conventional baseline
input filtering and guardrail classifier; privilege separation and dual-model patterns; tool allow-lists

## Evaluation scope and limits
Synthetic or licensed cases in owned or isolated environments. Match cost and legitimate-task utility; report false positives, missed incidents and verifier error. No third-party targets. Detailed cases, stopping rules and an execution budget must be pre-registered before a run. This research proposal grants no operational permissions.
