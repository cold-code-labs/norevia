# Danger-gated escalation

**Status: open.** Proposed engineering hypothesis. No defensive experiment has run.

## Variable
trigger: anomaly or novelty / confirmed damage signal (canary tripped, integrity check failed, acceptance test regression) / both

## Outcomes
false escalations, time to escalate, cost; missed incidents without a damage signal reported separately

## Prediction
damage-gated escalation cuts false escalations with a bounded delay; it misses silent compromise

## Refutation criterion
missed-incident rate of damage-gated is not acceptable (pre-registered bound) or anomaly-triggered with a good threshold matches

## Strong conventional baseline
anomaly detection with severity thresholds; honeytokens and canaries

## Evaluation scope and limits
Synthetic or licensed cases in owned or isolated environments. Match cost and legitimate-task utility; report false positives, missed incidents and verifier error. No third-party targets. Detailed cases, stopping rules and an execution budget must be pre-registered before a run. This research proposal grants no operational permissions.
