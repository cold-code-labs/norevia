> **Strict analogy.** Moons have no intention. The word "shepherd" is a metaphor for gravitational torque.

## Mechanism
- A ring of colliding particles spreads: collisions redistribute angular momentum, transferring mass inward and angular momentum outward; spreading can be slowed by gravitational interactions with satellites, and models must give survival times comparable to the age of the Solar System (Goldreich & Tremaine 1982, review text).
- Porco & Goldreich (1987, abstract): they examine kinematic relationships between the Uranian rings and all ten newly discovered satellites; propose that **1986U7 and 1986U8 are the inner and outer shepherds of the epsilon ring**, that 1986U7 is also the outer shepherd of the delta ring and 1986U8 an outer shepherd of the gamma ring; and compute the **resonant torques** the satellites exert, compared with the viscous torque and atmospheric-drag torque.

## Evidence
`natural-phenomenon` as interpreted by the authors; shepherding of Uranian rings is a published **proposal assessed on observations** — treat the full mechanism as author-reported.

## Engineering analogue
**A few light agents keep a crowd in shape:** a small number of boundary-keeping agents (rate limiters, schema gatekeepers) at the edges can hold a large population of workers within a band without controlling each one — at the price of continuous torque (monitoring effort).

## Limits of transfer
Gravity acts without messages or intent; the analogy is for confinement-by-boundary only. Not evidence about agents.
